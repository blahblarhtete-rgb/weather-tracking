Important — understanding your walking outlook
Weather information comes from Open-Meteo forecast data. It is not a direct reading from a weather station at every searched location. Mud and wet-ground ratings are estimates based on recent rainfall, forecast rainfall and available soil-moisture data. Wind exposure also varies with elevation and surrounding landscape. TrailWise does not currently verify path closures, flooding, landslips or actual ground conditions. Always check local notices and official weather warnings before setting out.
